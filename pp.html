Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Dwarka Collections, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect and how we utilize it to serve you better.

Information We Collect

To process your orders and handle seamless logistics, we gather the following basic details:

  • Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Delivery Address
  • Email Address (if explicitly provided)
  • Order Details & Screenshot References

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly utilized for core operational procedures, including to:

  • Process, verify, and confirm your incoming orders.
  • Deliver products safely through our courier networks.
  • Provide reliable post-purchase customer support.
  • Share structural order updates and occasional promotional offers.

Information Security

Your personal information is kept entirely secure. We maintain strict safeguards, and your data is **never sold, leased, or shared** with third-party tracking corporations. Information is exclusively revealed to registered courier fulfillment groups solely to handle physical parcel delivery services.

Cookies

Our website may deploy standard browser cookies to track general performance parameters, analyze traffic metrics, and improve overall website responsiveness and user experience.

Customer Rights

You retain the absolute right to control your information. You may contact our operations team at any time to update your personal records, request immediate corrections of inaccurate details, or ask any critical questions regarding your digital privacy.
