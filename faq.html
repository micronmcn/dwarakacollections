Yes, absolutely. Every outfit, saree, and fabric in our collection is 100% genuinely hand-woven by experienced rural weavers using traditional loom setups. We take pride in supporting ethical handmade fashion.
It is simple! Browse our collections, take a screenshot of your selected product, click on the 'ORDER NOW' button, fill in your details (Name, Contact No, and Delivery Address), upload the screenshot, and hit submit.
Once you submit your order form along with the screenshot, our team will verify stock availability and contact you directly via WhatsApp/Phone within a few hours to process your payment and finalize shipment.
For orders within Andhra Pradesh and neighboring states, it typically takes 3 to 5 business days. For pan-India domestic shipping, deliveries take around 5 to 7 business days depending on your specific location pin code.
Currently, because we work directly with rural weavers and deal with limited heirloom stock, we operate purely on a secure digital prepaid basis (UPI, Bank Transfer, or GooglePay). This allows us to keep our prices extremely fair.
As soon as your parcel is packed and handed over to our verified courier partner, we will automatically share the tracking link and consignment ID directly to your WhatsApp number so you can track it in real-time.
We accept exchanges only in cases of manufacturing defects or incorrect item deliveries. To secure an exchange, we kindly request our clients to record a continuous unboxing video of the parcel from start to finish.
Slight variations or minor knot textures are the native characteristics of fine authentic hand-loom weaving. These are not flaws; they are proof that the cloth was crafted completely by human hands, adding to its unique charm!
Yes, we do entertain custom color requests or wholesale orders for weddings and events. Please connect directly with our studio via our 'Contact Us' page or drop us a WhatsApp query at least 3-4 weeks in advance.
To maintain the vibrancy and longevity of the pure organic dyes, we highly recommend dry cleaning for the first couple of washes. For subsequent washes, hand wash separately in cold water using mild eco-friendly detergents.
